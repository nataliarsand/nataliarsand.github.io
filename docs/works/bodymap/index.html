BodyMap: Dosha Questionnaire

Designing a better way to understand your dosha imbalances

A playful yet grounded quiz experience inspired by traditional Ayurvedic wisdom, built for clarity, curiosity, and self-awareness. This passion project is my attempt to make ancient knowledge more intuitive (and beautiful) to explore. I designed, wrote, and built the entire React-based app—from translating traditional frameworks into user-friendly logic to crafting results with clarity and narrative flow.

Deliverables Collaboration

Deliverables

  • Structured self-assessment: Quiz format exploring patterns across body, mind, and habits
  • Smart results engine: Dual output logic for constitution and current imbalance
  • Modular content system: Thematic structure across digestion, movement, emotions, and lifestyle
  • Narrative insights: Friendly storytelling tone rooted in traditional principles

Collaboration

  • Self-initiated project with feedback from wellness peers and AI assistance

Design approach

  • Group-based questions: Instead of dozens of scattered questions, themes are clustered around how you feel, eat, move, and think.
  • Dual logic: The tool detects both your nature and your imbalance, gently revealing patterns without jargon.
  • Tone and language: It doesn’t feel like a spreadsheet or a mystic scroll. It feels like a thoughtful friend asking how you’ve been.

Desktop quiz interface

Mobile results page

Impact

  • Helped dozens of testers gain clearer insight into their patterns and tendencies
  • Received praise for feeling “honest”, “gentle”, and “more accurate than any dosha quiz I’ve ever done”
  • Set the foundation for future ideas: seasonal recalibrations, printable reports, and one-on-one integrations with practitioners

BodyMap is my way of bringing design to wellness without dumbing it down and inviting others to explore their dosha imbalances, one question at a time.
