### Project Details

The **Design Operations & Design System** initiative aimed to streamline design processes, create a unified design language, and improve cross-functional collaboration across Booking.com’s global teams. The project addressed challenges related to scalability and efficiency as the organisation expanded its product offerings and design teams.

My role involved leading the definition, development, and rollout of the **Booking.com Unified Interface (BUI)** design system while also establishing operational frameworks for design teams.

Key contributions included:

- Defining a **governance model** for the design system to ensure adoption and maintenance across teams.
- Creating a **component library** with reusable, accessible, and responsive UI components.
- Building onboarding and **training materials** to empower new designers and align cross-functional teams.
- Establishing collaboration frameworks between designers and developers to accelerate delivery.

By working closely with executive stakeholders, I ensured alignment between product goals and design priorities, fostering buy-in across multiple disciplines.

### Challenges & Solutions

1. **Scaling Design Across Teams**  
   Challenge: Ensuring consistency across Booking.com's growing design teams.  
   Solution: Built a centralised design system with robust documentation and a governance model to support adoption.

2. **Fostering Collaboration**  
   Challenge: Bridging gaps between designers, developers, and researchers.  
   Solution: Introduced workshops and processes that encouraged co-creation, ensuring all voices were represented in decision-making.

3. **Alignment Across Stakeholders**  
   Challenge: Securing buy-in from diverse stakeholders across departments.  
   Solution: Delivered iterative updates through demos and stakeholder alignment sessions, showcasing measurable progress.

### Outcome

- Implemented a **scalable design system**, used by 50+ teams across the organisation.
- Reduced **design-developer handoff issues** by standardising components and workflows.
- Accelerated **onboarding time** for new designers, reducing ramp-up by 30%.
- Increased **efficiency in design delivery**, enabling faster time-to-market for product updates.
