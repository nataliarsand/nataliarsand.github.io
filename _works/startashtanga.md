### Design approach

- **Accessible by default**: No prior yoga knowledge assumed. Clear language, gentle tone, welcoming design.
- **Community-first**: Open source from day one, with easy contribution paths for non-developers (content, translations, shala submissions).
- **Traditional with modern UX**: Respects the lineage while making information easy to navigate.

- Live at <a href="https://www.startashtanga.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">startashtanga.org</a>

- Growing community of contributors and practitioners
