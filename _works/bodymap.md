### Situation

Most dosha assessments fall into two camps: dense academic questionnaires requiring manual scoring and Sanskrit fluency, or oversimplified commercial quizzes gated behind email capture. Neither serves someone genuinely curious about Ayurveda. Most also conflate two distinct concepts: your lifelong constitution (Prakriti) and your current state of balance (Vikriti).

---

### Task

Create an automated, accessible dosha assessment that properly distinguishes constitution from current state, without requiring spreadsheets, manual scoring, or a Sanskrit dictionary.

---

### Action

I designed and built **BodyMap**, a dual-assessment tool with 40 themed questions organized by Body, Digestion, Mind, and Lifestyle. The conversational tone makes complex Ayurvedic concepts feel approachable.
