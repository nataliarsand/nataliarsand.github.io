### Context and Objective

I've been studying and applying Ayurveda for years, as a yoga practitioner, a curious human, and yes, someone who's a little witchy.

But every time I looked for tools to help people explore these concepts, I found the same thing: vague questions, unclear results, and designs that didn’t respect the depth or simplicity this wisdom deserves.

So I made my own.

---

### My Role

This was a hobby project, so I worked from sketching out the structure and writing the questions, to building the app and designing the experience. With a little help from AI, and feedback from a bunch of friends.

- Translated traditional frameworks into structured, user-friendly logic
- Designed a modular quiz interface that doesn’t overwhelm or condescend
- Wrote results with clarity, care, and narrative flow
- Built the whole thing in React with future extensions in mind

---

### Design Choices

- **Group-based questions**: Instead of dozens of scattered questions, themes are clustered around how you feel, eat, move, and think.
- **Dual logic**: The tool detects both your nature and your imbalance, gently revealing patterns without jargon.
- **Tone and language**: It doesn’t feel like a spreadsheet or a mystic scroll. It feels like a thoughtful friend asking how you’ve been.

---

### Impact

- Helped dozens of testers gain clearer insight into their patterns and tendencies
- Received praise for feeling “honest”, “gentle”, and “more accurate than any dosha quiz I’ve ever done”
- Set the foundation for future ideas: seasonal recalibrations, printable reports, and one-on-one integrations with practitioners

BodyMap is my way of bringing design to wellness without dumbing it down and inviting others to explore their dosha imbalances, one question at a time.
