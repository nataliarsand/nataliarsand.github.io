<section id="more-about" class="section-block">
  <h2>A bit more about me</h2>
  <p>I’m in love with design in all its forms. I studied both Graphic Design and Systems Analysis but most of what I know I learned by doing, living it, and doing the work.</p>

  <p>I started my career at 16 as a Webmaster at a small tech company in the countryside. My teenage years were all about learning everything I could about the internet.</p>

  <p>At 19, I joined a startup while waiting months to be old enough to sign the contract. I worked on the US platform and helped migrate to a new CMS using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and C#.</p>

  <p>After moving to the city, I got involved in tech communities and eventually joined Engage, a startup where I was first called a UXer. I worked on research, design, and front-end development.</p>

  <p>I then joined ThoughtWorks to go deeper into design and consulting in complex environments. From there, I moved to Booking.com to focus on data, research, and psychology in design.</p>

  <p>Today I’m a Lead Designer on the Design Systems team at Booking.com. Over nearly a decade, I’ve worked across B2B, B2C, led as a Design Manager in Design Operations, and now focus on scaling tools, processes, and systems that support our global design community.</p>
</section>
